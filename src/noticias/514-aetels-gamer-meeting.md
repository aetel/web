<p>Summer, with all those relaxing moments and lots of heat, has finally come. Some soda, a deck chair and internet connection is everything we need.</p>
<p>We think summer is too long and can get boring, why don’t you do something different? That’s why we invite you to our open meeting.</p>
<p>In this event we will play TeamFortress 2 by teams. For those who don’t know it, TF2 is a first person shooter, cooperative and exciting. It’s free to play in Steam, so you will only need to register to start playing. the event will take place in the Associations Hall down Block VII it ETSIST July, 31 in the afternoon.</p>
