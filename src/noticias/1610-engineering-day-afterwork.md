<p>The 5th of October at 17h we will celebrate the <strong>Engineering day Afterwork</strong> at Aula Magna ETSI Sistemas de Telecomunicación del Campus Sur – UPM (Crtra de Valencia, km 7).&nbsp;A meeting of various organisations with history, projects and a lot to celebrate:</p>
<ul>
<li>25 Years of&nbsp;<strong>AETEL- Asociación de Electrónica de Telecomunicación</strong>. Part of ETSIST (former EUITT). Electronic projects, Sphera congress, learning has characterised its existence.&nbsp;<a href="https://aetel.etsist.upm.es/">https://aetel.etsist.upm.es/</a></li>
<li><strong>IEEE Day</strong>: Global event that commemorates the first time electric engineers of all around the world got together to share their technics and ideas. <a href="http://www.ieeeday.org/%C2%A0" target="_blank" rel="noopener">http://www.ieeeday.org/&nbsp;</a></li>
<li>New management, new office and new projects at&nbsp;<strong>AMSAT EA</strong> – Radio Amateur Satellite Corporation Association. Technicians and amateurs of satellite communications. <a href="https://www.amsat-ea.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">https://www.amsat-ea.org/</a></li>
<li>10 years of&nbsp;<strong>Rama de Estudiantes Campus Sur</strong>: Closely related to AETEL and IEEE.</li>
<li>New team&nbsp;<strong>Young Professionals España</strong>– IEEE branch for newly formed engineers. In Spain it has 944 members.<a href="https://www.facebook.com/ypspain/%C2%A0" target="_blank" rel="noopener">&nbsp;https://www.facebook.com/ypspain/&nbsp;</a></li>
</ul>
<p>On this day we will unite different generations that have been part of these organisations. People with many years of experience and people who are just starting their university life.</p>
<p>Entrance is free. <strong>Registration is mandatory</strong> because venue’s capacity is limited. To register, please follow this <a href="https://events.vtools.ieee.org/m/177860" target="_blank" rel="noopener">link</a>. In the same webpage you’ll find information about the speakers attending the event as well as the positions they currently occupy.</p>
<p>In the final part of the event we’ll have a little afterwork with food and drinks and some surprises.</p>
