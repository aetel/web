<p>AETEL is pleased to present a Hardware Design Contest. The aim of the contest is to give praise to people’s personal projects; even those that end up in a drawer without nobody knowing them.</p>
<p>All the participants must present a document with the following parts:</p>
<ol>
<li>Name of the design.</li>
<li>How the idea came to them.</li>
<li>Problems that the design intends to solve.</li>
<li>Methodology and design steps.</li>
<li>All the schematics, images, explanations and calculations the participant considers compulsory for the understanding of the design.</li>
</ol>
<p>Originality, technical quality, documentation and design working state will be considered.</p>
<p>All the rules can be found <a href="http://www.etsist.upm.es/uploaded/963/Bases%20Concurso%20de%20Diseño%20de%20Proyectos%20Hardware.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">here</a>.</p>
