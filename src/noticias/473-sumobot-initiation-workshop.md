<p>October 20 is the registration deadline for our Sumobot Initiation Workshop, organised by two students from the ETSIST and AETEL members, Pablo Martínez and Pablo de Miguel.</p>
<img src="/assets/img/noticias/473-sumobot.jpg" alt="sumobot">
<p>This course is aimed at those who want to know and build their own sumobot (competition rules, etc), also want to know more about the Arduino platform (programming, configuration, etc) and its interaction with the different sensors and other electronic devices.</p>
<p>All the necessary materials will be provided with the course so every participant can build and program his/her own sumobot:</p>
<ul>
<li>1 Arduino Nano</li>
<li>2 Servos</li>
<li>2 Wheels</li>
<li>1 Proximity Sensor</li>
<li>2&nbsp;Reflexive Sensor</li>
<li>1 3D Printed Chasis (each participant can create his/her own design if he/she wishes to do so)</li>
<li>Battery holder</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>The workshop will have two parts:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>4 Tutorials&nbsp;available&nbsp;online</li>
<li>4h tutor time&nbsp;(to determine, in the month of November)</li>
</ul>
<p>Participants will be informed via email.</p>
<p>Those interested can come to our headquarters at the Hall of Associations in Block VII, ground floor or by mail: <a href="mailto:aetel@euitt.upm.es">aetel@euitt.upm.es</a></p>
<p>Entries to the workshop, whose price is 20 € for members and 30 € for non-members will be made in AETEL HQ.</p>
